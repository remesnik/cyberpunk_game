# Meatspace audio provenance

`rooftop_wind.wav`, `distant_traffic.wav`, and `night_electric.wav` are original synthesized prototype assets generated by `tools/art/generate_city_layers.py`. These three assets and their generator are dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0. They contain no third-party samples and may be used without payment or attribution. Each file is a 60-second, mono, 16-bit PCM loop at 11025 Hz with a quiet seam.

`city.wav` and `utility.wav` are existing repository synthesis from `tools/art/generate_meatspace_audio.gd`. They remain unchanged.

`train.wav`, the car horn files, and the root-level train-generation files were already supplied/modified in the working tree before this pass. Their contents are preserved; the CC0 dedication above does not relicense them. The train is used as the existing project asset. Unwired car horn files remain untouched.
